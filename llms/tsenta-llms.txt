# Tsenta

> Tsenta is a Y Combinator-backed transparent agent that watches 50,000+ company career pages, tailors your résumé per role, and submits applications across 19 ATSes (Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Rippling, iCIMS, BambooHR, Workable, JazzHR, Jobvite, BreezyHR, Oracle Cloud, SmartRecruiters, Paylocity, UltiPro, ADP, Dover, Gem, Zoho Recruit). Available on web, native iOS and Android apps, desktop, iMessage, WhatsApp, Chrome extension, and as an MCP server / CLI for any AI agent.

Every change Tsenta makes is shown to you before it goes out. You can watch each application happen, or run headless and check the recording later.

## Who Tsenta is for

Tsenta is built for active job seekers — software engineers, product managers, designers, and other professionals — running a high-volume search across multiple companies. It's especially useful for candidates who want to get ahead of the applicant flood that hits a posting 48 hours after it goes live, and for those applying to companies that use complex ATSes like Workday where manual form-filling takes 20–45 minutes per application.

## How it works (four stages)

- [Find](https://tsenta.com/#workflow): Tsenta watches 50,000+ company career pages. The moment a role drops that fits your résumé, you know, and you are already in the top 100 applicants. Roles surface hours before LinkedIn or Indeed index them.
- [Prep](https://tsenta.com/#workflow): Per-role résumé and cover letter, generated from the actual job description, with keyword alignment, ATS-safe formatting, and every change shown to you before send.
- [Apply](https://tsenta.com/#workflow): Tsenta opens the form. Tsenta hits submit. Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and 15 more ATSes; login, every field, open-ended questions in your voice, document uploads. Watch it happen or run headless.
- [Track](https://tsenta.com/#workflow): Recruiter emails land back on the right application. Status moves on its own; your inbox stops being a graveyard and starts being a pipeline.
